## The challenge

Eastern Gravels supplies premium aggregates (granite, sharp sand, and gravel) to builders and developers in Lagos. Most orders came in by phone and word of mouth. Customers had no easy way to see products, check what was available, or place an order outside business hours.

There was a second audience too: Nigerians living abroad who are building homes in Lagos. They need a supplier they can trust from thousands of kilometres away, and they need to order without a phone call at an awkward hour.

## The approach

We started with how customers actually buy aggregates. Site engineers and contractors want quick answers: what's available, how much, and when can it arrive. They are usually on a phone, on site, on mobile data.

So the site had to be fast, plain, and built around one action: **order on WhatsApp**. Every product page sends a prefilled message with the product and quantity, so the conversation starts with the details already there.

For the design, we used an industrial orange and charcoal palette that fits the trade and stands out from generic construction templates.

## The solution

- **Next.js and Tailwind** for a fast, mobile-first site that loads quickly on mobile data
- **Supabase** for product data the team can update without a developer
- **WhatsApp ordering** on every product, with prefilled order details
- **A diaspora section** for Nigerians building from abroad, explaining how ordering, payment, and delivery work remotely
- **Full SEO infrastructure**: clean metadata, structured data, a sitemap, and pages targeted at local Lagos search terms

## What's next

We continue to support the site, and we are adding content that helps builders compare aggregate types and plan quantities for their projects.
